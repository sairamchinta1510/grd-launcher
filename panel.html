👆 Gesture Reference


☝️
Drag — move mouse cursor (relative, like a trackpad)
👆
Tap — left click at cursor position
✌️
Two-finger tap — right click
✌️
Two-finger drag — scroll
🤏
Pinch — zoom remote desktop in/out (0.5× – 3×)
🖐️
Three-finger swipe up — open toolbar
⬆️
Swipe from bottom edge — open toolbar
⬇️
Swipe toolbar down — close toolbar
